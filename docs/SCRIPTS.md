# Stand-off scripts, by country

Three beats before every level: the General's ultimatum, the defender's
refusal, the General's last word. The defender speaks his own language where
the writer put it there; the General never understands a word of it.

Lines live in `src/game/cast.js` under `STANDOFF`; the cast (ranks, names,
nations, portraits) is `CAST` in the same file. All nine are the writer's.

Player: **Gen. Buck Hollister**, United States.

## United Kingdom — Westminster, the Elizabeth Tower

Defender: **F.M. Sir Reginald Pomp**, United Kingdom. Level id `westminster`.

- US: Give it up you limey bastard
- UK: God save the queen, and your soul
- US: Thank god, I was worried we dragged all this damn ordnance across the Atlantic for nothing!
