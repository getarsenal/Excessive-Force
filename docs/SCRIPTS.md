# Stand-off scripts, by country

Three beats before every level: the General's ultimatum, the defender's refusal (in his own language where the writer put it there), and the General's last word — he never understands a syllable of the reply.

Lines live in `src/game/cast.js` under `STANDOFF`; the cast (ranks, names, nations, portraits) is `CAST` in the same file. The first four levels' lines are the writer's; the five second-half levels are drafts written in-house and marked as such.

The player's commander on every level: **Gen. Buck Hollister**, United States.


## United Kingdom — London, Elizabeth Tower

Defender: **F.M. Sir Aubrey Pell**, United Kingdom. Level id `westminster`.

- **US:** Give it up you limey bastard
- **UK:** God save the queen, and your soul
- **US:** Thank god, I was worried we dragged this ordnance across the Atlantic for nothing!

## France — Paris, Eiffel Tower

Defender: **Maréchal Gaspard Thibault**, France. Level id `paris`.

- **US:** Surrender or die Frenchie
- **FR:** Va t'étouffer avec une baguette
- **US:** All I heard was baguette, and that's all I need

## India — Agra, Taj Mahal

Defender: **Maharaja-Gen. Arvind Rathore**, India. Level id `agra`.

- **US:** Nice tomb. Vacate the premises before I add to the occupancy.
- **IN:** Twenty thousand men built this in twenty-two years. You have four guns and a hangover.
- **US:** Twenty-two years? Give me twenty-two minutes.

## Egypt — Giza, the Great Pyramid

Defender: **Mushir Tarek El-Masry**, Egypt. Level id `giza`.

- **US:** God it's hot out here, give it up before we all melt would ya?
- **EG:** اطلع الهرم ده وهاتك يا طفل يا تخين
- **US:** I don't speak Sanskrit habibi, but I get the gist. Someone get me some damn water and a lot of bombs

## Mexico — Chichén Itzá, El Castillo *(draft)*

Defender: **Gral. de División Rodrigo Salazar-Quiroz**, Mexico. Level id `chichen`.

- **US:** Step aside, chief. We do not negotiate with calendars.
- **MX:** Llevas mil años tarde. Esto ya lo enterramos una vez y lo volvimos a construir encima.
- **US:** Built it twice, huh? Well hell, that is half my work done for me.

## Italy — Pisa, the Leaning Tower *(draft)*

Defender: **Generale Ottavio Brambilla**, Italy. Level id `pisa`.

- **US:** That thing is already falling over. Save us both the ammunition.
- **IT:** Pende da ottocento anni, americano. Tu non duri otto minuti in questo sole.
- **US:** Eight hundred years of leaning. Let us call it a running start.

## Russia — Moscow, Saint Basil's Cathedral *(draft)*

Defender: **Marshal Lev Zimyanin**, Russia. Level id `moscow`.

- **US:** Nine churches. I brought enough for ten.
- **RU:** Иван ослепил зодчего, чтобы он не построил второго. Тебе глаза оставим — смотри.
- **US:** I did not catch a word of that, but he sounded real confident about it.

## Australia — Sydney, the Opera House *(draft)*

Defender: **Gen. Angus Kerrigan**, Australia. Level id `sydney`.

- **US:** You are on a point with water on three sides. Where exactly are you going?
- **AU:** Mate, you have got one road in and we have got the whole harbour. Take your shot.
- **US:** Finally, one of them speaks English. Does not make him right.

## Brazil — Rio de Janeiro, Christ the Redeemer *(draft)*

Defender: **Marechal Joaquim Duarte-Ribeiro**, Brazil. Level id `rio`.

- **US:** Seven hundred metres up with no road out. Walk down or be carried.
- **BR:** Ele está de braços abertos há um século. Nem por você ele abaixa.
- **US:** Arms out like that? Son, that is not a welcome, that is a target.
