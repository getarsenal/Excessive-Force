# Hungarian Parliament, Budapest — the proportion table

Filled in from three reference photographs and `python3 tools/survey.py budapest`
before the builder is written; checked against `node tools/blocks.mjs budapest` and
`node tools/postcard.mjs budapest` after.

The survey carries the House as one forty-three point outline named Országház,
129 × 265 m, with the dome on the level's origin, the river façade forty metres
west of it and the east front seventy-five metres east; the wings run north
and south along the Danube and the pavilions at their ends stand a little
wider than the wings. Nothing else stands within 120 m: the House has its own
bank.

Stones: 16,863 at low, 26,600 at high; 0 loose at both. Sections: northwing
and southwing 4,712 each, hall 4,630, drum 1,136, dome 1,019, spires 654.

Kind of problem: shell with wings. The twist: the wings are the mass and not
the contract; the dome stands on the sixteen piers of its own drum, which run
down through the hall to the ground, and the bar is the dome, the drum and the
two spires. Open the drum at hall level and the dome comes down in a sheet.
