# Neuschwanstein Castle, Schwangau — the proportion table

Filled in from three reference photographs and `python3 tools/survey.py neuschwanstein`
before the builder is written; checked against `node tools/blocks.mjs neuschwanstein` and
`node tools/postcard.mjs neuschwanstein` after.

The bake cuts the ridge with its summit at 965 m ASL, level (±1.5 m) over
about 100 × 200 m running north–south, the first shelf 45 m down, the second
70 m down, the valley 170 m below. The survey names the castle's six
buildings: the Palas 58 × 46 at 45 m (to the ridge) at the west end of the
courtyard, the Torbau 21 × 33 at 22 m at the east end, the Ritterhaus 26 × 16
along the north side, the Kemenate 26 × 16 along the south, the Viereckturm
12 × 12 at 35 m on the north side. The courtyard runs east–west across the
ridge, so both ends overhang it: the plan is shifted fifteen metres west so
the gatehouse stands on the rock and the Palas hangs over the gorge, on
sixty metres of footing.

Stones: 7,806 at low (1,305 under the summit), 12,717 at high; 0 loose at
both. Sections: palas 4,534, northtower 782, squaretower 605, knightshouse
454, bower 382, gatehouse 187.

Kind of problem: cantilever cluster on a ridge. The twist: two slender
towers on the corners of a big block, the round one 98 m on a shaft fourteen
metres across; they go over the way they lean, and the Palas beside them is
the counterweight that does not.
