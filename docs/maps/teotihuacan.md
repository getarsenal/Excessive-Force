# Pyramid of the Sun, Teotihuacan — the proportion table

Filled in from three reference photographs and `python3 tools/survey.py teotihuacan`
before the builder is written; checked against `node tools/blocks.mjs teotihuacan` and
`node tools/postcard.mjs teotihuacan` after.

The survey: one outline, `Pirámide del Sol`, 276 x 273 m — the pyramid with
its Adosada and the platform round the foot — centred 75 m WSW of the origin,
on level ground 2,200 m east-west and from 256 m north to 743 m south of the
origin at 2,300 m. Nothing else within 120 m.

Bodies (real m): h 16 / 14 / 12 / 11 / 12, taluds 13.6 / 11.9 / 10.2 / 9.4 / 10.2,
treads 8; summit 34 m square. The cave: a void 3.2 m wide and 4.2 tall through
the lowest body from the west foot to a four-lobed chamber 14 m across under
the centre, with seven `CHARGE` blocks walled in along it and over the chamber.

Stones: Sun 16,705 at low / 33,013 at high; Moon 2,150 / 3,837; 0 loose at both.

Kind of problem: mountain, Giza's kind. The twist: nothing falls and it is
quarried, but the cave under the west face is real and the charges are along
it — digging in from the stair pays where shelling the skin does not.
