# Santa Maria del Fiore, Florence — the proportion table

Filled in from three reference photographs and `python3 tools/survey.py florence`
before the builder is written; checked against `node tools/blocks.mjs florence` and
`node tools/postcard.mjs florence` after.

The survey has the cathedral as a 29-point outline 156 × 97 m: the façade at
x −32, the nave 42 m over its buttresses to x 48, the crossing's centre at
x 76, the north and south tribunes 37 m wide and the east tribune's tip at
x 124. The Campanile di Giotto is a 14 × 14 m square at (−26, 29), 85 m in the
bake; the Battistero an octagon 38 × 34 at (−80, −2). Seventy-nine buildings
stand within 120 m; the ground is level to ±1.5 m for 450 m every way.

Kind of problem: a shell. It never topples. The twist: the dome bears on the
drum, the drum on the eight piers at the crossing's corners and the four
great arches between them; take the piers at one corner out and the drum
loses its bearing on that side, and the dome above comes down into the
crossing in a sheet. The tribunes wrap the piers: the way to a pier is through
a tribune.
