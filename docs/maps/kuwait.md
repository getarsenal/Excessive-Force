Kind of problem: a cantilever, three of them. The twist: the mass is the
spheres and the shafts are slender — cut a shaft below a sphere and the sphere
comes down whole with everything above it; cut above it and you have only
taken the needle off.

Measured: 6,726 stones at low, 20,790 at high, 0 loose at both. The shaft
flares into a solid collar under each ball, a step of less than a stone a
course, so the ball stands on the whole section: on a plain 1.6 m ring the
main tower's lower sphere crushed the course under it.

Engine note: the bake puts the origin 4.5 m under water (Ras Ajuza is land),
so the levelled pad is a white island in the bay and the podium stands a
step above the sea.
