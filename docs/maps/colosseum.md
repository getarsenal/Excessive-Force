# Colosseum, Rome — the proportion table

Filled in from three reference photographs and `python3 tools/survey.py colosseum`
before the builder is written; checked against `node tools/blocks.mjs colosseum` and
`node tools/postcard.mjs colosseum` after.

The survey has the Colosseo as a 24-point outline, 188 × 142 m, centred 14 m
east-north-east of the origin with its long axis 14° south of east, and
nothing else within 120 m: the piazza is open ground. The ground under it is
level to ±1.5 m across the whole span (the Oppian rises to 48 m 300 m north,
the Palatine to 46 m 400 m west).

| | The real thing | Built (scale 1.5) |
|---|---|---|
| Plan, from the survey | 189 × 156 m (outline 188 × 142) | 283 × 234 m; 270 × 217 above ground with three fifths of the outer ring gone |
| Height from the ground the camera stands on | 48 m outer wall, ~38 m inner ring | 72 m outer, 57 m inner |
| Taller than wide? | no — a ring | no, 0.33:1 |
| The bottom third is | eighty arches on piers, one storey of them | eighty arches: 4.4 m openings between 2.4 m piers, with half-columns |
| Dominant surface colour (as a material) | travertine, pale warm grey | LIMESTONE outside, BRICK for the inner ring, RUBBLE for the cavea fill (77% of the visible volume, seen only from above) |
| Where the eye goes | the tall outer wall against the low broken ring beside it | the same: 32 standing bays on the north, the brick ring at 57 m on the south |
| Outbuildings the survey names, and which are built | none within 120 m | none |

Tiers as built: 10.5, 11.9, 11.6 m arcades and a 13.7 m attic with pilasters and a
square window in every other bay; the inner ring 14 m inside the outer face, two
arcades and a plain wall to 38 m; radial walls every fourth bay tie the two
rings under the standing arc; the cavea is twelve rings of fill stepping from
the 4.5 m podium to 30 m at the inner ring; the hypogeum's brick walls stand in
the 87 × 55 m arena.

Kind of problem: a shell, a ring — broken bay by bay, never toppled. The twist:
the outer wall is eighty piers carrying three storeys of arches with nothing
behind them where the cavea has gone; take two adjacent ground-tier piers out
and the bay above them comes down outward. Where the radial walls survive
they tie the outer ring to the inner and the same shot brings down less.
