# Atomium, Brussels — the proportion table

Filled in from three reference photographs and `python3 tools/survey.py atomium`
before the builder is written; checked against `node tools/blocks.mjs atomium` and
`node tools/postcard.mjs atomium` after.

The survey has the Atomium as a 51-point hexagonal footprint 99 × 97 m, 102 m
high, centred three metres from the origin, with a vertex (a lower sphere) on
the bearing 12° south of east; three service sheds of 6 m within 120 m and
nothing else. The Heysel is flat to ±1.5 m across the whole span.

Structure as built: spheres as horizontal rings whose inner radius always
reaches past the course below's outer face, solid caps; tubes as corbelled
stacks of four yawed stones per course, starting on a ring's course top and
ending on a ring's course bottom; the column from the ground through bottom,
centre and top; two legs under each lower sphere; windows as omitted stones
just above each sphere's equator, where the garrison stands in the shell.

Kind of problem: a lattice. The twist: the mass is in the spheres and the
spheres carry only themselves; the load runs down the column and the bipods.
Take the bipods and it is a tower on one leg; take the column at the pavilion
and the whole molecule comes down.
