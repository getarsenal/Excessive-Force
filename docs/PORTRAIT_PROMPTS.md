# Officer portraits: the prompt template

The forty-nine drawn officers (`public/assets/characters/*.png`) are painted
illustrations, not something a script can draw. What *can* be templated is
the brief that produced them, so the placeholders can be replaced
in the same style by anyone with an image generator. Every drawn portrait
shares these properties; keep them all.

## The template

> Full-figure caricature of a **{rank}** of the **{nation}** army, standing,
> **arms folded, chin up, reads as a refusal**. Head to boots in frame, slight
> low angle. **{look}** A national flag patch on the shoulder and one national
> emblem somewhere on the uniform. Exaggerated jaw and brow, cigar or pipe
> optional, weathered face, grey at the temples. Highly detailed painted
> illustration, hard ink outlines, rich saturated colour, dramatic rim light,
> **plain black background** with a soft dark halo behind the figure. No text,
> no watermark, no weapon pointed at the viewer.

Output: 1024 × 1536, the figure cut out onto transparency (RGBA), with the
soft dark halo left in the alpha so the figure sits on the stand-off's blue
without a hard edge. File name and slot are in `manifest.json` under
`enemies.<level>`; the `look` line there is the `{look}` in the prompt.

## Installing one

Drop the PNG over the placeholder of the same name in
`public/assets/characters/`; nothing else changes. The placeholder is a
tinted silhouette generated by the script in the session notes, kept only so
no stand-off shows an empty slot.
